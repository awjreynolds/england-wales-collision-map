# West of England Road Collision Evidence

Evidence about reported road injury collisions and the people injured in them across four local authorities.

## Language

**West of England study area**: Bristol, Bath & North East Somerset, South Gloucestershire and North Somerset. This geographic scope does not define combined-authority membership.

**Collision**: A reported STATS19 personal-injury road collision. One collision can injure several people.

**Casualty**: A person killed or injured in a reported collision; casualty severity describes that person's injury.

**Collision severity**: The most severe injury classification assigned to a collision, distinct from the severity of each casualty.

**Fatal collision**: A collision classified as fatal; its count is not a count of people killed.

**Serious collision**: A collision classified as serious, excluding fatal collisions.

**Slight collision**: A collision classified as slight, excluding serious and fatal collisions.

**KSI casualties**: People killed or seriously injured, counted from casualty records. Fatal and serious collisions are not KSI casualties.

**Road-user involvement**: Evidence that a pedestrian, cyclist or motorcycle user was involved in a collision; absent evidence is not automatically evidence of absence.

**Collision concentration**: A spatial grouping of reported collisions. Frequency alone does not establish exposure-adjusted risk.

**Persistent collision location**: A collision concentration observed across multiple years under the observatory's stated grouping and minimum-count rules.

**Exposure**: The amount of travel or road use against which a collision frequency could be compared.

**Intervention**: A dated change to a defined part of the network. A before/after association alone does not establish its causal effect.
